WHO WE ARE

Making research and data work for organisations.

A research, data analytics and digital solutions company providing technical expertise and institutional support to organisations working with data, evidence and complex decision-making.

Research expertise. Practical implementation.

We combine research methodology, statistical science, data engineering and technology to help organisations strengthen their research processes, improve data quality, measure programme performance and translate information into actionable insights.

We work with NGOs, academic and research institutions, government agencies, development programmes and private-sector organisations.

Our approach goes beyond delivering reports. We build analytical systems, develop practical tools, strengthen institutional capacity and provide ongoing technical support that enables organisations to use their data more effectively.

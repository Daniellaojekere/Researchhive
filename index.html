KNOWLEDGE HUB

Insights

Notes on research methodology, drawn from projects we work on.

Correlation vs. causation

Two variables moving together doesn't mean one causes the other — check for confounders before recommending policy.

Three common questionnaire errors

Double-barrelled questions, loaded or leading questions, and overlapping response categories.

Why Cronbach's Alpha isn't enough

Alpha assumes tau-equivalence, which rarely holds. Many journals now also require McDonald's Omega.

Choosing the right test for Likert data

Likert scales are ordinal — use Mann-Whitney U instead of a t-test for 2 groups, Kruskal-Wallis + Dunn's for 3+.

METHODOLOGY NOTES

Short notes from our practice.

The standards behind our analysis, in brief.

Correlation vs. causation

Two variables moving together does not mean one causes the other. Identify plausible confounders and the design that would rule them out before recommending policy.

Three common questionnaire errors

  1. Double-barrelled questions
  2. Loaded or leading questions
  3. Overlapping response categories

Why Cronbach's Alpha isn't enough

Alpha equals reliability only when items are (essentially) tau-equivalent. With unequal loadings it usually underestimates. Check dimensionality first, then report omega from a fitted factor model.
